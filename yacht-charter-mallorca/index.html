Private yacht charter in Mallorca

Limitless is a 21.5-metre Maiora, based at Club de Mar in Palma and looked after by a dedicated crew of two. She is a private charter — just your group, your dates and the stretch of coast you feel like — whether that is a half-day swim in the nearest turquoise cove or a week around the Balearics.

One boat, entirely yours

We only run one yacht, and only ever for one group at a time. That means the day is built around you: your pace, your music, your idea of a good time on the water. Up to 12 guests by day and 7 overnight, with the crew handling the boat, the anchoring, the beach-club reservations and every other detail so you can focus on the people you are with.

What's included, and what isn't

Every charter includes the crew, cleaning and VAT — no hidden extras bolted on at the end. Catering and beach clubs are arranged on request, and on multi-day trips provisioning, mooring and fuel run through a transparent APA with a running tally. A quick message tells you exactly where you stand before you commit; nothing is held back.

On board

An outdoor bar with four fridges and an ice maker, a spacious sun deck, a Seabob, paddleboards, snorkel gear, a lily pad and a jet tender, Bose and Sonos sound throughout, air conditioning and smart TVs below. Everything you need for a day on the water is already aboard.

Questions

How many guests can Limitless take?

Up to 12 guests for a day charter and 7 sleeping aboard on multi-day trips.

Is the crew included?

Yes — a professional crew of two, plus cleaning and VAT, are included in every charter. Gratuity is never expected.

Where does Limitless depart from?

Club de Mar in Palma de Mallorca.
