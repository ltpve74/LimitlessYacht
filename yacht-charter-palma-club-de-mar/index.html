Yacht charter from Club de Mar, Palma

Limitless is berthed at Club de Mar, one of Palma's classic marinas, right on the edge of the city and the old town. It means you can be sipping a coffee in Palma one moment and anchored over turquoise water the next — the nearest coves are barely half an hour away.

A perfect base

From Club de Mar the whole southwest coast is on your doorstep: Portals Vells and its sea caves, the Illes Malgrats marine reserve, the sheltered blue of Cala Llamp — all within an easy morning. Point the other way and the south coast's white-sand beaches and Cabrera's national park open up for a full day. Wherever you start from, the crew meets you at the boat and takes it from there.

Easy from anywhere in Palma

The marina is a short taxi from anywhere in the city and the cruise-port and airport are close by, which makes Limitless an easy choice whether you are staying in Palma, out at Portals, or just off a flight.
