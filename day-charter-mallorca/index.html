Day charter in Mallorca

A full day aboard Limitless is the most popular way to see the coast — enough time to reach two or three coves, swim in properly clear water, have lunch on board and be back for a sunset drink at the marina.

Check dates WhatsApp the crew Call +34 643 678 072

Captain and crew usually reply within the hour, and always within 24 hours. Crew and VAT included. Gratuity is never expected.

How a day unfolds

You board at Club de Mar in the morning and the crew shapes the route around you and the weather — the sheltered turquoise bays of the southwest for an easy day, or a longer run down to Es Trenc, Sa Dragonera or Cabrera when the forecast is settled. Swim off the platform, take the Seabob and paddleboards out, and let the day slow right down. It is your boat for the day; there is no schedule but your own.

Lunch and the details

Catering is arranged on request — anything from a relaxed spread to a proper long lunch — or bring your own. The crew handles anchoring, tender runs ashore and any beach-club reservations. Crew, cleaning and VAT are included; you'll have a clear quote before the day.

Questions

How long is a full-day charter?

Typically 8–10 hours. Shorter 4- and 6-hour options are on the half-day page.

Which coves can we reach in a day?

The southwest bays easily; Es Trenc, Sa Dragonera and Cabrera on settled-weather full days. The crew will advise for your date.
