Ibiza & Formentera — the white isle crossing

Cross to the Pityusic islands — Ibiza's energy and Formentera's Caribbean sandbanks.

West of Mallorca lie Ibiza and, just beyond it, Formentera — the pair that draw everyone eventually. Ibiza for its glamour, its anchorages and its evenings; Formentera for water so shallow and clear over white sand that it looks tropical.

The crossing is around four and a half hours, so this is firmly a multi-day trip: cross with settled weather, spend your days on Formentera's sandbanks and your evenings off Ibiza, and come back when the wind is right. We can take a marina berth or hang on anchor depending on what the group wants. As with any longer cruise, the crew will lay out distances, fuel, mooring and the APA clearly for your dates before you commit — nothing held back.
