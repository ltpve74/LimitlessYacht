Portals Vells — sea caves and turquoise coves

The easy first bay — clear water and cool sea caves, barely half an hour from the marina.

Twenty minutes past the marinas of the southwest and the coast softens into a run of shallow, impossibly clear bays. Portals Vells is the one nearly everyone loves first: sheltered, sandy-bottomed and calm, with the old stone sea caves carved into the headland at one end.

It is the shortest hop we make — around 35 minutes from Club de Mar — which makes it the perfect anchor for a half-day. Swim straight off the swim platform into water you can see your toes in, take the tender or a paddleboard across to the caves, and let the crew handle the rest. Because it is so close, there is no rush: a morning here and lunch on board, or a lazy afternoon and a swim as the light goes gold.
