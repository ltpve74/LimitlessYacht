Menorca is the Balearic that time forgot, in the best way: a biosphere reserve of undeveloped coves, pale sand and prehistoric shores, with far fewer boats than its noisier neighbours.

The crossing is a little under four hours, making it a rewarding multi-day escape for guests who want somewhere genuinely quiet. Work your way along the south coast's string of wild calas, drop into the vast natural harbour of Maó, and swim in water that stays empty even in August. Distances, fuel, mooring and the APA are mapped out honestly for your dates — the crew would rather over-explain the costs than surprise you.
