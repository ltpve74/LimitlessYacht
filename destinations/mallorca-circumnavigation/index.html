Around Mallorca — the full-island circumnavigation

The complete island, coast to coast, at your own pace over several days.

Mallorca is far more varied than any single day can show: the turquoise shallows of the south, the wild Tramuntana in the northwest, the quiet coves of the east. The circumnavigation strings them all together into one unhurried loop.

It is roughly 200 nautical miles — about twelve and a half hours of actual cruising — so we recommend five to seven days to do it justice, sleeping aboard and waking in a new bay each morning. You set the pace; the crew shapes each day around the weather and what you feel like. Provisioning, mooring and fuel on a trip this length run through an APA (advance provisioning allowance) with a transparent running tally, so there are no surprises at the end. The best way to see the whole island.
