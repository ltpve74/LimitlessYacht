Es Trenc — Mallorca's Caribbean-white sand

The island's most famous stretch of wild, white sand and unreal turquoise water.

Es Trenc is the one people picture when they imagine Mallorca from a postcard — a long, undeveloped run of Caribbean-white sand backed by dunes, with shallow water that turns every shade of turquoise and jade.

Because it is protected from building, the beach stays wild, and the best of it is only really reachable comfortably by boat. We anchor off the sand in the shallows, and the whole group can float, swim and paddle in warm, waist-deep water for as far as you like. At around an hour and a half each way it is a classic full-day run down the south coast — bring nothing but a swimsuit and let the crew handle lunch.
