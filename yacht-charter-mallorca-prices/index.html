Charter rates

Low season — half-day (4h) from €1,700 · 6 hours from €2,400 · full day from €3,000. High season (July–August) — half-day (4h) from €2,200 · 6 hours from €3,100 · full day from €4,000. Crew and VAT are included in every rate.

What's included

Your professional crew of two, cleaning and VAT are all part of the price. Also aboard at no extra cost: the Seabob, jet tender, paddleboards, snorkel gear, sound system and outdoor bar. A refundable security deposit (20%) applies.

What's extra

Catering and beach-club reservations are arranged on request. On multi-day charters, provisioning, mooring, extra fuel and the final cleaning run through a transparent APA with a running tally. Crew gratuity is never expected — entirely at your discretion.

Questions

No. Crew, cleaning and VAT are included; catering and multi-day running costs are shown transparently before you book.
