Half-day and sunset charters

Not every day on the water needs to be a whole day. A half-day aboard Limitless — four or six hours — is time enough for a morning swim in a nearby cove, a lazy afternoon, or a sunset cruise out of Palma bay with a drink in hand.

Captain and crew usually reply within the hour, and always within 24 hours. Crew and VAT included. Gratuity is never expected.

Short, but not rushed

Because our home bays are so close — Portals Vells is barely 35 minutes away — a half-day still buys you real time in the water. A morning charter for a swim and a paddle before lunch; an afternoon that drifts into evening; or a sunset run when the light goes gold over the bay and the coast empties out.

Included

Crew, cleaning and VAT are part of the price, and the sound system, water toys and outdoor bar are all yours for the session. Drinks and catering on request.

Questions

How long is a half-day charter?

Can we do a sunset cruise?

Yes — an evening half-day timed for sunset over Palma bay is one of the loveliest ways to use the boat.
