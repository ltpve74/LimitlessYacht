Season guide

The best time to charter in Mallorca

Mallorca is a genuinely long season on the water. Here is how the months actually feel from the deck, so you can pick the trip that suits you — and Limitless is available year-round.

Captain and crew usually reply within the hour, and always within 24 hours. Crew and VAT included. Gratuity is never expected.

May, June, September, October — the sweet spot

Warm, settled seas and softer light, with the coves far quieter than mid-summer. For many guests these shoulder months are the best of all: the water is swimmable, the anchorages have space, and the longer runs down to Cabrera or across to the islands are easy on calm days. Low-season rates apply.

July & August — peak summer

The liveliest, warmest months, with the longest days and the busiest coves. It is Mallorca at full tilt — book early, as our calendar fills quickly, and expect high-season rates (July–August).

A note on the weather

The crew always shapes the day around the forecast — where you go on any given day depends on the wind, and they'll steer you to the most sheltered, beautiful water available. It is why the routes are a starting point, not a fixed plan.

Questions

When is the best time to charter in Mallorca?

May, June, September and October offer warm, calm, quieter conditions; July and August are the liveliest and busiest.

Is Limitless available all year?

Yes — available year-round, with low-season and July–August high-season rates.
