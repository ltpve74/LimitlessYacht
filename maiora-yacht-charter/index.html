Limitless — a 21.5 m Maiora

Limitless is a Maiora flybridge motor yacht, 21.5 metres of warm, well-kept space built for exactly the kind of days Mallorca does best. She is maintained to charter standard and run by a dedicated crew of two who genuinely love the sea.

Captain and crew usually reply within the hour, and always within 24 hours. Crew and VAT included. Gratuity is never expected.

Built for the coast

Twin MTU engines move her comfortably at a cruising 16 knots, so the coves are never far, and there is room to spread out: a generous flybridge and sun deck up top, elegant outdoor seating and an outdoor bar with four fridges and an ice maker aft, and a cool, air-conditioned interior below. She takes up to 12 guests by day and sleeps 7.

The toys

A 330 jet tender, a Seabob, a stand-up paddleboard, snorkel sets and a lily pad; Bose hi-fi and Sonos control throughout, smart TVs, and a swim ladder and platform that make getting in and out of the water effortless.

The crew

Two on board who handle everything — catering, beach-club reservations, the anchoring and all the details — attentive and experienced, and part of a small team who share equally in whatever recognition guests choose to give. Gratuity is never expected.

Yacht at a glance

Builder: Maiora · Length: 21.5 m × 5.6 m beam · Engines: 2 × MTU, 1,500 HP · Guests: 12 day / 7 overnight · Crew: 2 · Home port: Club de Mar, Palma de Mallorca. You will not confuse her with the 96 m Lürssen also named Limitless that sometimes berths in the same marina. This page is the charter yacht.
