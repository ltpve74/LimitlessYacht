Multi-day and overnight charters

Give it more than a day and Mallorca opens right up — and Menorca, Ibiza and Formentera come within reach. On a multi-day charter you sleep aboard, wake somewhere new each morning, and let the trip find its own rhythm.

Captain and crew usually reply within the hour, and always within 24 hours. Crew and VAT included. Gratuity is never expected.

Where you can go

Island-hop the wild Tramuntana coast in the northwest, cross to Formentera's turquoise sandbanks and Ibiza's anchorages, slip over to quiet Menorca, or take the full circumnavigation of Mallorca over five to seven days. Up to 7 guests sleep aboard; the crew builds each day around the weather and what you feel like doing.

Honest about the costs

Longer trips run through an APA — an advance provisioning allowance, typically around 10% of the charter fee — covering provisions, extra fuel and mooring, with the captain keeping a transparent running tally throughout. Anything left at the end comes back to you. We'd rather over-explain the numbers than surprise you; a quick message gets you a clear breakdown for your dates.
